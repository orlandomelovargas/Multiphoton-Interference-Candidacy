# Multiphoton Interference and Representation Theory

This repository contains the documents and computational materials associated with the doctoral candidacy examination **“Multiphoton Interference and Representation Theory in the Many-Photon Limit.”**

The research focuses on the relations between multiphoton interference with partial indistinguishability, the semiclassical behavior of irreducible representations of (U(M)), and classical multiport interferometry in the many-photon limit.



## Repository Contents

The repository includes three main files:

**PhD_Candidacy_Examination.pdf** — The main doctoral research proposal. It presents the physical motivation, theoretical framework, results obtained so far, research objectives, methodology, and open problems.

**`Mathematical_Appendices.pdf`** — The mathematical derivations supporting the proposal. Appendices A–I develop the exact probabilistic formulation, the Schur–Weyl decomposition, the geometry of compatible unitary frames, the construction of irreducible representations and weight spaces, Gram matrices of projected states, physical sectorial density matrices, stationary conditions and Hessians, and the derivation of the sectorial classical map. The appendices also state the assumptions required for the corresponding asymptotic results.

**`Multiphoton_Interference_Calculations.wl`** — The companion Wolfram Language code used for symbolic and numerical calculations. It includes routines for constructing compatible frames, evaluating Gram matrices and physical sectorial densities, computing detection probabilities and reduced Hessians, and generating the numerical results and figures presented in the documents.

## Computational Information

The calculations were developed and tested using **Wolfram Mathematica 14.5**.

The numerical results complement the analytical derivations. They illustrate the behavior of the corresponding mathematical constructions but do not replace the assumptions and proofs required for the asymptotic statements.
